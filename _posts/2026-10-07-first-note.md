Hello, and welcome. I'm setting up this site as a compact home for work, experiments, and writing. My interests sit around computer graphics, rendering, and the tools that make visual ideas easier to build and understand.

Here I plan to share finished projects as well as notes from the process: a sketch that changed direction, a technical detail worth remembering, or a result that taught me something. I prefer writing things down while the work is still fresh.

This first post is a starting point. The projects page will grow as I choose what to share; in the meantime, the code and updates live on GitHub. Thanks for stopping by.
